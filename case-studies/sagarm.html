Life On Earth

We rarely talk about what is actually happening in the ocean.

We lay internet cables, extract oil, and move massive ships across countries.
These activities slowly damage — and take away — the only thing marine life has

“Their Home”

A hit-and-run accident.

But instead of humans, coastal wildlife are being injured or killed.

We came across a dataset showing ship activity in the ocean, including commercial and fishing vessels.
This raised an important question:

How much impact do these ships have on the ocean and the marine life beneath it?

The answer revealed that the impact is not limited to large marine animals like whales and sharks. It also affects smaller and more fragile ecosystems such as shellfish, small fish, and coral.
